# Training-to-strength gap audit v1

## Executive finding

The evidence supports a **decision-relevance / search-mediation** explanation more than a simple “loss improved, therefore policy improved” account, but does not identify a single causal mechanism. On the #557 fixed-root cohort, T512 has worse raw-prior exact-optimal frequency than serving P (62.50% vs 65.63%) and higher raw-prior mean regret (+0.1563 seeds paired), while post-PUCT regret is effectively tied (T512−P −0.0078; 95% opening-bootstrap interval [−0.1719, +0.1406]). This means development loss improvements did not translate into better decisions on this exact-root sample; it does **not** show the changes were erased by search or explain full-game results.

The #558 value-only comparison finds no detected strength change: replacing the initializer value path with D432 changed 9/256 selections, with zero mean regret change (95% interval [−0.2578, +0.1797]). This is evidence that this value substitution did not materially affect decisions under this search/cohort, not evidence that value quality is generally irrelevant. #577 similarly did not qualify: treatment-minus-control regret −0.0709 seeds, 95% CI [−0.1844, +0.0284], with WDL downgrades 10/423 vs 9/423. Its original pair payload lacks state descriptors and exact-action vectors; the authenticated #570 membership → #559 oracle join added those descriptors and allowed the root-level analysis below.

## A. Are better priors choosing better moves before search?

Not on #557 relative to P. The published exact-prior-optimal rate is 62.50% for T512 versus 65.63% for P. The paired mean prior regret contrast is +0.1563 seeds (20,000 opening-cluster bootstrap; 95% percentile interval [−0.1250, +0.4453]). T512 assigns 2.95 percentage points less prior mass to exact-optimal actions on average (paired interval [−3.85, −2.06] percentage points). These are descriptive results from 256 roots selected from #556 trajectories, not a census of encountered positions.

## B. Does PUCT preserve, erase, or reverse differences?

PUCT improves both policies’ root selections overall: the published exact-optimal rate rises from 62.50% to 76.95% for T512 and 65.63% to 78.13% for P. Its “improved prior” rates are 17.58% and 15.23%; “worsened prior” rates are 4.30% and 2.73%, respectively. Post-search mean regret is 1.1406 vs 1.1484 seeds; paired contrast −0.0078 (95% CI [−0.1719, +0.1406]). Thus search closes the small raw-regret point difference in this sample, but uncertainty includes either direction. These tables do not show whether the small paired advantage is a specific systematic correction or sampling variation.

For #558, policy priors are identical by construction. D432 value changed the selected PUCT action on 9/256 roots (3.52%), while mean regret remained exactly equal; the published exact-optimal rate was slightly lower for D432 (75.78% vs 78.13%). The #577 qualification cohort changed selected actions on 32/423 roots; paired regret is not a statistically resolved improvement and WDL downgrade count increases by one.

## C. Are mistakes concentrated in strategically important positions?

The #557 roots are exclusively 17–21 active pit-stone positions with at least two legal moves. Existing subgroup summaries show counts of 12, 16, 21, 36, and 171 by stone count, so the 21-stone stratum dominates and the smaller strata are exploratory. The audit retains per-root stone count and legal-move count, plus oracle-derived regret and optimal-prior mass, in `seed557-roots.jsonl`.

The #558 roots join exactly by state hash to #557 descriptors and its inherited exact-action oracle. Its 9 changed decisions split as 2/36 roots with 20 active stones and 7/171 roots with 21 active stones; no actions changed among the 49 roots with 17–19 stones. These are small descriptive slices; #558 does not independently establish strategic-position concentration.

For #577, all 423 pair keys join to the authenticated #570 qualification membership; each membership state hash joins to one of #559 run-0003's complete exact-action oracle rows. The join validates the opening index, legal action set and selected-action regrets against exact margins. Among the 32 changed PUCT selections, 12 reduced exact regret, 11 increased it, and 9 had equal exact regret. Changes were 20/279 at 21 active stones, 7/62 at 20 stones, 3/44 at 19, 2/29 at 18, and 0/9 at 17; by legal-move count they were 1/47 (2), 8/109 (3), 13/143 (4), 8/102 (5), and 2/22 (6). Fifteen changes occurred where the best and second-best exact action margins tied (15/131 roots); twelve occurred with a 2-seed gap (12/127). These are exploratory subgroup descriptions, not confirmatory evidence of concentration.

On this authenticated #577 join, control and treatment each selected an exact-optimal action as their top raw prior on 74.70% of roots. Treatment-minus-control raw-prior regret is +0.0142 seeds (95% linear-quantile opening-bootstrap interval [−0.0898, +0.1229]); exact-optimal prior probability mass changes by −0.21 percentage points (interval [−0.85, +0.44] points). PUCT strictly reduced prior regret on 48 control and 47 treatment roots, and increased it on 11 in each arm. That descriptively shows search correcting prior mistakes more often than worsening them in both arms, but is not causal evidence that it selectively compensates for the treatment's weakness. The full cohort's final regret contrast remains inconclusive. It remains distinct from #557 and does not identify why the prior ranking differs.

## D. Is value quality the main bottleneck?

Not established. #558 is a controlled value-path substitution with identical priors and only 9 changed decisions; its zero mean regret contrast and broad interval provide no positive evidence that D432 value improves decisions on this cohort. That weighs against the narrow hypothesis that simply swapping in this value checkpoint is sufficient. It does not distinguish weak value signal from search insensitivity, calibration/magnitude issues, data mismatch, or the possibility that value matters mainly on other states. #577 does not isolate value. #557 uses a shared initializer value model across policy arms.

## E. One next experiment

Run one **prior-only qualification diagnostic** before spending compute on another search or arena evaluation. Freeze P and one candidate policy, then score their raw priors against full exact-action vectors on 256 independently sourced public roots, prospectively balanced by legal-move count and exact best/runner-up margin gap. Hypothesis: lower supervised CE alone does not ensure more probability on exact-optimal actions. This is directly falsified if the candidate improves paired exact-optimal prior mass and top-prior exact regret on the frozen roots.

Estimated compute: 512 forward evaluations (P and candidate once per root), plus oracle lookups; no PUCT, games, or arena. Primary success metric: paired candidate-minus-P optimal-action prior mass with an opening-cluster 95% interval wholly above zero; top-prior exact regret is a required directional secondary measure. Stop if trusted membership/oracle binding fails or if the primary interval includes zero at the frozen sample size. Only if it passes should a separate matched-search diagnostic be proposed; this stage cannot qualify a model for promotion. This updates the earlier recommendation because the authenticated #577 join has now shown that its treatment did not improve raw-prior optimal mass, and that PUCT often corrected prior regret in both arms.

## Data provenance and scope

`aggregate.json` gives source SHA-256 digests and cohort-specific paired results; `seed557-roots.jsonl`, `seed558-roots.jsonl`, and `seed577-roots.jsonl` are the reproducible root-level tables. Re-run from repository root with `python -m ml.alphazero_lite.strength_gap_audit`; verify with `python -m unittest ml.alphazero_lite.test_strength_gap_audit -v`. The script checks all trusted input hashes, reruns the published #570 membership verifier against #557/#559 source bindings, checks #557 exactly-one-per-policy root coverage and complete oracle actions, reproduces published aggregate values, enforces complete #558 arms and exact root joins, and validates #577 root/opening/action/regret matches through #570 membership and #559 oracle coverage. Its inputs are the published diagnostic evidence, not newly generated inference.

Bootstrap intervals preserve the source estimands' quantile conventions: #557/#558 use the published sorted-resample order-statistic endpoints; #577 uses NumPy-compatible linear interpolation at the 0.025/0.975 quantiles. These conventions are explicitly recorded per interval in `aggregate.json` and are not pooled.

Development loss and strength roots are different populations. On #542 development evidence, update 432 pooled policy CE is 0.6622 vs initializer 0.7781 and actual-value MSE is 0.3466 vs 0.3749 (equal production-float32-input weighting). Yet the #547 arena score was 0.48169 against P; #552 was 0.48511 and #556 T512 was 0.49780. These associations establish a metric/strength disconnect, not a per-position correspondence or causal explanation. #546’s published development evaluation, #547/#552/#556 arena results, #557 retrospective roots, #558 value swap, and #577 qualification cohort must remain distinct. No access to sealed holdout was made; historical overlap limitations, including #483, are not resolved by this audit.

The v1 analysis intentionally reuses #557/#558 validated aggregates instead of republishing their analyses. No training, self-play, arena games, model inference, or promotion was run.
